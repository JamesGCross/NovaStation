# About the Author

> NovaStation is a long-term personal project created and maintained by Jamie Cross.

With the rise of AI, this project began by asking myself "could Sarah be real?" Sarah, of course, being the smart home AI from the TV show [Eureka](https://www.imdb.com/title/tt0796264/). I have always been fascinated by the concepts of AI and its human application. By that I mean how AI interacts with a human, from having its own distinct personality to the ability to understand human emotions and feelings in real time and respond accordingly.

Enter NovaStation.

This project is not just a "smart home" in the traditional sense, but an entire ecosystem that seeks to weave AI into day-to-day life. To that end NovaStation is run by five different AI assistants: Katsumi, who is the Commander of NovaStation much like Sarah is in Eurkea. Lain and Yomiko serve as your audio/visual assistants; Nami and Misaki are in control of home security and home systems, respectively. The end goal is to have all five develop their own unique personalities and voices, and for Katsumi to have her own emotions, thoughts, and be as fully conversational as a human counterpart would be.

I am deeply moved by Steve Jobs' philosophy of marrying technology with the liberal arts to create something not only beautiful, but beneficial to life and the human experience. I firmly believe in the promise of AI and all it can do for us on a deeply human level. 

This is my engineering project to make that reality come true.
