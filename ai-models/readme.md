# AI Models - NovaStation

NovaStation utilises a distributed AI architecture consisting of specialised assistants:

- Katsumi (Core AI / Commander)
- Lain (Music Systems)
- Yomiko (Media Systems)
- Nami (Security Systems)
- Misaki (Infrastructure / Browser / Diagnostics)

Each assistant operates as an independent service with their own Call Word and may optionally accessed and operated by Katsumi.
